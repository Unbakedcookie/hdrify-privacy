Privacy Policy for HDRify

Effective date: October 7, 2026

HDRify ("the app") is made by Unbaked Studios. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: everything stays on your phone. The app has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics, and it does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your photos or data anywhere.

What the app uses, and why

Camera. The app takes the photos that it stitches into 360° images and HDRIs. The photos are stored inside the app on your phone.

Motion sensors. The app reads your phone's orientation while you capture, so it knows which direction each photo faces. These readings are stored with the capture, on your phone.

Location (optional). If you allow location access and "Add location to photos" is on in Settings, the app records where a capture was taken: latitude, longitude, altitude, accuracy, the time of the reading and the compass heading. It is saved with the capture and written into the 360° photo's metadata, so services such as Google Maps can place the photo. You can turn this off in Settings at any time, or turn on "Remove location when sharing" to share copies without it. The app works fully without location access.

Notifications (optional). Used to tell you when a stitch is finished or has failed. Nothing about you is collected.

Settings. Your choices in Settings (for example dark mode) are stored on your phone.

What leaves your phone

Nothing leaves your phone unless you do it yourself:

  • Saving to Pictures. By default, finished 360° photos are saved to the Pictures folder on your phone. Other apps that can see your photos, such as a gallery or a cloud backup app (for example Google Photos), may then show or back them up according to their own settings and privacy policies. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Sharing and exporting. When you share a photo or export an HDRI or raw files, they go to the app or place you choose, and that app's or service's own privacy policy applies from then on.

The developer never receives your photos, your location or anything else from the app. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google on your phone, Google Play may give the developer anonymous crash and performance reports; that is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, not by the app.

Storage and deleting your data

Captures are kept inside the app until you delete them in the Library. Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored inside it. Photos you saved to Pictures and files you exported stay where you put them until you delete them yourself. The app's own data is excluded from Android's backup and device-to-device transfer.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if in-app purchases are added through Google Play), this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change.
